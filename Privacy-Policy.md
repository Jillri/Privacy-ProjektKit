# Privacy Policy for ProjectKit

Last updated: October 4, 2026

ProjectKit respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles data when you use ProjectKit on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Data We Collect

ProjectKit does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties.

The app is designed to store your projects, notes, tasks, sketches, moodboards, diagrams, widgets, color themes, and related app content locally on your device and, if enabled, in your private iCloud account.

## iCloud Sync

ProjectKit uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to sync your app data across your devices.

Your synced app data is stored in your private iCloud account and is handled by Apple’s iCloud infrastructure. The developer of ProjectKit does not have access to your private iCloud data.

You can manage iCloud settings in the Settings app on your device.

## Sign in with Apple

ProjectKit may use Sign in with Apple to help identify your app profile across Apple devices.

When using Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a unique user identifier and, depending on your Apple settings, your name or email address. ProjectKit does not use this information for tracking, advertising, or sharing with third parties.

## In-App Purchases

ProjectKit offers optional in-app purchases, such as paid color themes.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. ProjectKit does not receive or store your payment details.

## Data Sharing

ProjectKit does not share your personal data with advertisers, analytics providers, or third-party data brokers.

## Analytics and Tracking

ProjectKit does not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking.

The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Children’s Privacy

ProjectKit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data for the developer, no personal information from children is knowingly collected, sold, or shared.

## Data Deletion

You can delete your projects and app content directly inside the app.

If iCloud sync is enabled, deleting app data may also remove synced data from iCloud, depending on your iCloud settings and sync state.

You can also manage or delete iCloud data through Apple’s iCloud settings.

## Security

ProjectKit relies on Apple platform security, local device storage, and iCloud security to protect your data. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Apple ID, device passcode, and iCloud account.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
